Package Tours
AP Tourism Development Corporation (APTDC) has been conducting local and surrounding temple package tours every day from Tirupati for the convenience of Pilgrims.

Local Temples Tour

Local temple tours are conducted at the frequency of 1 hour from 06:00 hrs to 13:00 hrs from Srinivasam Complex opposite to APSRTC Central Bus Station, Tirupati and at a frequency of 2 hours from 06:00 hrs to 12:00 hrs (4 trips) from Vishnu Nivasam Complex opposite railway station, Tirupati. These tours cover:

• SRI PADMAVATHI AMMAVARI TEMPLE, TIRUCHANOOR
• SRI KALYANA VENKATESWAA SWAMYVARI TEMPLE, SRINIVASA MANGAPURAM
• SRI AGASTHEESWARA SWAMYVARI TEMPLE, THONDAWADA
• SRI KAPILESWRASWAMYVARI TEMPLE, KAPILATHEERTHAM, TIRUPATI
• SRI GOVINDARAJA SWAMYVARI TEMPLE, TIRUPATI.
• SRI VAKULAMATHA TEMPLE, TIRUPATI.

Note :
  • The duration of the tour is 5 hours and fare charged is Rs.200/- per pilgrim.

Surrounding Temples Tour
Surrounding temple tours are conducted at 8 AM and 9 AM (2 trips) from Srinivasam Complex opposite to APSRTC Central Bus Station, Tirupati. These tours cover:
• SRI VENUGOPALASWAMYVARI TEMPLE, KARVETINAGARAM
• SRI KALYANAVENKAESWARASWAMYVARI TEMPLE, NARAYANAVARAM
• SRI PALLIKONDESWARA SWAMYVARI TEMPLE, SURUTUPALLI
• SRI VEDANARAYANASWAMYVARI TEMPLE, NAGALAPURAM
• SRI KARIYA MANIKYA SWAMYVARI TMPLE, NAGARI
• SRI KASI VISESWARA SWAMYVARI TEMPLE, BUGGA
• SRI PRASANNA VENKATESWARASWAMYVARI TEMPLE, APPPALAYAGUNTA

Note :
  • The duration of the tour is 8 hours and the fare charged is Rs.500/- per pilgrim.

For further information on package tours please contact 0877-2289123 & 9848007033.
